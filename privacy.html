Data Security & Retention Standards

We implement defense-in-depth security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • Cryptographic Protocols: End-to-end TLS 1.3 encryption for all data in transit across HTTPS, secure webhooks, and REST endpoints. Encryption at rest for databases and backups.
  • Access Controls: Principle of least privilege (PoLP), multi-factor authentication (MFA) on internal environments, and continuous access logging.
  • Data Retention Windows: Notification transmission logs are retained only as long as necessary to confirm delivery, resolve delivery errors, and meet audit standards (typically 30–90 days), after which telemetry is purged or irreversibly anonymized. Account information is maintained while an account remains active or until deletion is formally requested.

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (including India, the European Union under GDPR, and the UK), you possess recognized rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You may request an export of the personal information we hold concerning you.
  • Right to Rectification: You may request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete details.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request the permanent deletion of your account records and notification contact details, subject to lawful retention necessities.
  • Right to Revoke Consent: You can withdraw consent for notification delivery at any time with immediate effect.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at noxwing.official@gmail.com with your request and verifying details. We respond promptly within 30 days.
